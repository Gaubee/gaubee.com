    <div class="x-arch-text x-arch-orig">I've been using GLM-5.4 for a while and I can confidently say that it's the best Open Model you can use right now.<br /><br />It places itself at #7 on KingBench 4 which is the #1 Open Model placement.<br /><br />From what I can tell, the model is an honest effort and isn't very benchmaxxed or stuff like that and it has good vibes (just don't look at the reasoning tokens... you feel sad for the model at times)<br /><br />As much as I know it's a looped transformer with 700B params and a technical report shall come later.<br /><br />It's the most careful model that I have ever used... It has found out many bugs and security vulns in my apps that others haven't (incl. Fable). That's not say that it's the best either. The model takes a lot of time to reach a conclusion to a task especially the Max effort can make it spiral down for a while.<br /><br />It is on average about 4x slower than Neon on Max reasoning effort. At Medium reasoning effort this does indeed but at the cost of performance. High is the best sweet spot for me.<br /><br />It isn't slow because of TPS btw.. KingBench 4 took 431 minutes against Neon's 109. Each step isn't slower (22.9 s, against 26.7 s for Neon); it just takes far more of them, 1,128 against 244. It also reasoned 1.04M tokens against Neon's roughly 0.07M. About three-quarters of its time is spent generating and a quarter running builds and tests.<br /><br />I wouldn't recommend you to just use this model but keeping it as a reviewer for work and making it work on more complex architecture or searching for bugs, security vulnerabilities is really worth it imo.<br /><br />This is a model that you can use end-to-end on work without much issues but it isn't the best or SOTA. <br /><br />I have it in my council of reviewers at all time before shipping anything and I am really happy about that.<br /><br />How it behaves:<br /><br />It reasons on almost every step. Under 1% of its steps have near-zero reasoning, against 14–39% for the GPT models. Max gives about 1.5× the reasoning of High.<br /><br />It overthinks. On a security task, High and Max both hit the 180 s timeout while still thinking, and Medium passed in 95 s.<br /><br />Tool errors are mid-pack.

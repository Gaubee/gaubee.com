Open source. Free for everyone. <br /><br />Faster and more capable than hyperframes or remotion.  (10-20x faster with real footage and complex scenes).<br /><br />And it renders without a browser….<br /><br />Capable of more than just video. It’s built on <br />@BevyEngine<br /> so it’s highly capable at producing 3D art, shaders and games. <br /><br />It’s also built on Blitz Dom <br />@dioxuslabs<br /> , and a bridge between the page and the 3d world behind it allows you and your agent to write everything in typescript and react… without a browser. <br /><br />This video below was made entirely with Claude, Raea, and a weekend. I never had more fun playing and toying with creative work than I did making this. <br /><br />But to be honest, that’s not where I’m stopping. I want Raea to be an engine that correctly empowers creatives and agents, while not cognitively offloading the parts that make your work unique. <br /><br />I’m excited to continue working on this project as long as I can, and always open to feedback!</div>
